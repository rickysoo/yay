Step 3

Use it

Tell your AI what you want to make. For example:

"Use You-AI-You to write a cover letter."
"Use You-AI-You to reply to a customer."
"Use You-AI-You to plan a lesson."

Answer its questions with real facts. Check the draft. Change anything that isn't true or doesn't sound like you.
